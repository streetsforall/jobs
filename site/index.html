Streets For All

Get hired to fix LA's streets.

The people who design, plan and build our streets work for public agencies. This board collects open planning, engineering, outreach, GIS and project management jobs across LA-area agencies, updated every morning.

    Listings come straight from each agency's official job site and link back there to apply. Civil service jobs usually require an exam, so read the bulletin carefully and apply before the filing deadline. Spotted a job that belongs here, or one that doesn't? Tell us in the #jobs channel on Slack.
